1. What MaudeDash is

MaudeDash is a free, open-source analytics tool for the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's Manufacturer and User Facility Device Experience (MAUDE) database. It reformats publicly released FDA data and computes descriptive and inferential statistics over it. The software is published under the MIT License; the underlying FDA data is a work of the U.S. Government and is in the public domain.

3. The nature of the underlying data

You must understand the following before relying on any output. These are properties of MAUDE itself, not shortcomings of this tool.

  • There is no denominator. MAUDE records reports, not exposures. The number of devices in use is unknown, so no failure rate, incidence, risk or probability can be derived. Percentages shown by MaudeDash are shares of reports.
  • Reporting is voluntary for many sources and incomplete for all of them. Report volume reflects reporting behaviour, regulatory attention and publicity at least as much as device performance.
  • Reports are unverified. A report reflects what a submitter stated. The FDA does not confirm that the described event occurred as reported, nor that the device caused it.
  • Reports may be duplicated, amended or withdrawn, and the FDA revises the database retroactively. Figures change over time.
  • Disproportionality signals are hypothesis-generating only. A statistical signal is a prompt to investigate, never evidence of causation, defect, negligence or liability.
